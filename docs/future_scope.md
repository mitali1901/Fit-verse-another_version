# FitVerse – Future Scope (Version 2 Development Roadmap)

> **Important Scope Note:** Version 1 strictly delivers the first 50% of the project as a fully working local MVP (Authentication, Dashboard, AI Yoga Studio with 3 poses, Real-Time Red/Green correction, Photo-based posture analysis, Gemini AI Assistant, User Profile, and MongoDB integration). The items below represent the modular Version 2 roadmap.

---

## 1. Expanded Pose Library (10+ Postures)
- **Trikonasana (Triangle Pose)**
- **Bhujangasana (Cobra Pose)**
- **Utkatasana (Chair Pose)**
- **Ustrasana (Camel Pose)**
- **Phalakasana (High Plank)**
- **Setu Bandha Sarvangasana (Bridge Pose)**
- **Vashisthasana (Side Plank)**

## 2. AI Personalized Fitness Planner
- Automated workout schedule generation based on user fitness assessment.
- Progressive overload and flexibility milestones.

## 3. AI Nutrition & Meal Planner
- Macro and micronutrient meal recommendations.
- Photo-based meal calorie estimation via Gemini Vision.

## 4. Holistic Lifestyle & Wellness Tracking
- **Sleep Quality Tracking:** Sleep cycle logs and bedtime meditation reminders.
- **Water Intake Tracker:** Daily hydration target and smart notification logs.
- **Mindful Breathing (Pranayama) Studio:** Audio-visual pacing for Box Breathing and Anulom Vilom.

## 5. Gamification & Community Challenges
- Milestone achievement badges ("7-Day Consistency Warrior", "Alignment Master").
- Community streaks and peer challenges.

## 6. Long-Term Analytics & Export
- Monthly progress reports with posture improvement trends.
- PDF export for yoga teachers and physical therapists.
